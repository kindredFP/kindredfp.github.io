Who We Are

For the Love
of the Game

JP Albano is the Founder and Head Trainer of THE LAB Basketball. His journey began overseas after a tournament recruiter spotted his championship performance — he later earned a Mythical Five award during his rookie professional season. When he returned to Toronto, his passion turned to giving back.

JP moved to Milton and became Head Coach of a youth rep team in 2014, earning them a Division 1 (AAA) ranking — the province's highest standard — by mid-season, and winning multiple championships including AAU tournaments in New York and Pennsylvania.

Seeing a gap in Milton's training landscape — no private or small-group facility existed — JP opened THE LAB Basketball to give athletes the personalized reps and attention they deserve.

Ball Handling

Master the most fundamental weapon in basketball. Athletes work through a series of 1-ball and 2-ball handling drills designed to build touch, confidence under pressure, and the ability to read a defence.

1-ball isolation patterns 2-ball coordination drills Stationary defender reads Agility ladder integration Pylon weave sequences Speed dribble development
Ages 8 and Up

Shooting Clinic

Efficient catch-and-release mechanics, proper shooting form from various spots on the floor, and advanced methods to create space off the dribble into a quality shot.

Catch & release mechanics Off-dribble shooting Form correction & reps Mid-range development Footwork & balance Game-speed repetitions
All Levels Welcome

Finishing at the Rim

Learn unique, creative ways to attack the basket. Get comfortable finishing with both hands, through contact, and with an array of layup packages that high-level defenders can't predict.

Floaters & runners Contact finishing Euro-step variations Reverse layup packages 1-on-1 vs trainer And-one situations
Ages 10 and Up

Defence & Basketball IQ

Learn how to stop your opponent just as effectively as you can beat yours. JP teaches defensive positioning, reading screens, and in-game decision-making that translates directly to wins.

On-ball defence stance Help-side positioning Jab step reads Triple threat decisions Pick-and-roll coverage 1-on-1 full-length
Ages 12 and Up
