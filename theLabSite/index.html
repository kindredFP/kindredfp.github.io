Who We Are

For the Love
of the Game

JP Albano is the Founder and Head Trainer of THE LAB Basketball. His journey began overseas after a tournament recruiter spotted his championship performance — he later earned a Mythical Five award during his rookie professional season. When he returned to Toronto, his passion turned to giving back.

JP moved to Milton and became Head Coach of a youth rep team in 2014, earning them a Division 1 (AAA) ranking — the province's highest standard — by mid-season, winning multiple championships including AAU tournaments in New York and Pennsylvania.

Seeing a gap in Milton's training landscape — no private or small-group facility existed — JP opened THE LAB Basketball to give athletes the personalized reps and attention they deserve.

THE LAB works with athletes from 8 years old up to the university and professional level, both boys and girls. Programs are available for all skill levels — complete beginners through high-level rep players. Groups are separated by age and skill level based on enrollment.

Private (1 athlete): Fully customized 1-on-1 with Coach JP. Maximum attention, fastest development.

Semi-private (2–5 athletes): Small group with personalized feedback, competitive reps, and a lower cost per session.

Group (6–9 athletes): Sold in packages of 10+ sessions. Team concepts, competitive drills, and conditioning. Separated by age or skill as needed.

Drop-in availability is limited and subject to scheduling. Please contact us or call 416-433-0408 to check current drop-in options. We recommend booking a package to secure your regular spot and lock in pricing.

Use the forms in the Register Now section — there are separate forms for new players, returning players, and 3-on-3. Payment must be made immediately after submitting to officially hold your spot. Schedule details are noted on each form.

Proper athletic wear and basketball shoes are required. Bring a water bottle and a ball if you have one — equipment is available on-site. Sessions are high-intensity, so come fuelled and hydrated. Be ready to work hard.
